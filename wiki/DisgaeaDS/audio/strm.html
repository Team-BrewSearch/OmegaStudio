STRM is Nintendo's DS streamed audio format, played from storage in blocks rather than held in memory. In Disgaea DS the 173 .strm files in msgvo.dat are the only streams; music uses SSEQ instead. File extensions in this game do not always match the format inside, so check a file's magic before trusting its name.

Specification notes

  • In a STRM, the last-block length at 0x38 does not include padding; the data as a whole is padded to 4 bytes at the end, and the DATA size and file size count that padding.
  • A STRM stores its loop start in samples at 0x20.

In OmegaStudio

STRM sounds play in the Audio pane. PCM8, PCM16 and IMA-ADPCM decode to 16-bit WAV, and Replace sound from WAV encodes a WAV back in the sound's own codec. The extracted WAV stops at the sample count in the header.
