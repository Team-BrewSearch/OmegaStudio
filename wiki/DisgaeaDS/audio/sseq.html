SSEQ is Nintendo's DS sequence format: a compiled stream of note and control commands, usually built from a text source. All music in Disgaea DS is SSEQ. A sequence plays through an SBNK and its SWAR, and all three travel together in a DSEQ file.

Specifications

  • NitroStudio2: SSEQ
  • FeShrine: SDAT and SSEQ
  • TCRF: SDAT and SSEQ

In OmegaStudio

An SSEQ plays through its SBNK and SWAR. The sequence player runs the DS sound driver: 16 tracks and 16 voices, tempo, envelope, LFO and sweep, and renders to WAV via Extract Audio. Replacing the SSEQ, SBNK or SWAR updates playback. The DSEQ word at 0x2C is the length to the first loop point in milliseconds.
