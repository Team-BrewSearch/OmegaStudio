SWAV is Nintendo's DS single-sample format: one PCM8, PCM16 or IMA-ADPCM wave with its rate and loop point. In Disgaea DS sound effects and voice clips in se.dat and voice.dat are SWAV, stored under both .se and .swav names; the four yrah_.swav files in se.dat are empty.

Specification notes

  • SWAV data is padded to whole 4-byte words.
  • A loop offset counts 4-byte words from the start of the data: the ADPCM header word plus the loop start divided by the samples per word (8 for IMA-ADPCM, 4 for PCM8, 2 for PCM16). A start between words rounds down.
  • Every shipped non-looping ADPCM wave has offset 1, which is the same formula at sample 0.
  • A STRM stores its loop start in samples at 0x20.
  • Loops: an extracted WAV carries a smpl chunk with one loop (start, and the last sample as end) when the sound loops. On import, a WAV with a smpl loop sets the loop flag and start, and samples after the loop end are dropped, since the DS loops from the end of the data; a WAV without one clears the loop flag.

In OmegaStudio

SWAV and STRM sounds play in the Audio pane. PCM8, PCM16 and IMA-ADPCM decode to 16-bit WAV, and Replace sound from WAV encodes a WAV back in the sound's own codec. Re-importing an extracted WAV gives back the original file byte for byte.
