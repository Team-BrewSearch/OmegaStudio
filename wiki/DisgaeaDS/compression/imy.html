IMY is Disgaea DS's compressed image format, used for nearly every texture and screen: map tiles, effects, UI and title art.

Header (32 bytes)

OffsetSizeTypeDescription
0x004u320
0x044u32Unpacked size
0x084u32Row bytes
0x0C2u160
0x0E2u16Flags
0x101u8Bits per pixel
0x111u80
0x122u16Height
0x142u16Colour count
0x1616u8[16]0

After the header comes the BGR555 palette, then the pixels. Flag bit 0 means stored; otherwise a u16 info-byte count, the info bytes and the data follow, and the scheme is the PS2 one documented on Netherworld Research, working in 16-bit units with look-backs of 2, the row, the row plus 2 and the row minus 2. 4bpp pixels are low nibble first. Palette bit 15 is opacity: transparent sprites clear it on index 0.

Specification notes

  • IMY replacement keeps each pixel's old index when its colour has not changed, because palettes repeat colours.
  • New colours must fit the palette size; otherwise the palette is rebuilt.
  • The image is refused when it uses more colours than the IMY holds.

In OmegaStudio

An IMY shows in the Texture pane and can be replaced from a PNG of the same size. Replacement keeps each pixel's old index when its colour has not changed; new colours must fit the palette size, otherwise the palette is rebuilt, and the image is refused when it uses more colours than the IMY holds.
