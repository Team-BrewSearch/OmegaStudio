Shrek Smash n' Crash Racing (Torus Games, 2006) on GameCube, PlayStation 2 and PSP. The engine is RenderWare 3.6 with Torus wrappers; every chunk header carries the library stamp 0x1803FFFF.

Games

  • GameCube (USA, EUR)
  • PlayStation 2 (USA, EUR)
  • PSP (USA, EUR)

File Formats

    Conventions

    • Chunk headers are RenderWare structs. The size in the header does not count the 4-byte endian marker that precedes the struct payload.
    • Model, animation and geometry details are read from the GameCube USA main.dol and checked against every shipped .trm and texture.
    • Transparency follows RenderWare's rule: blend whenever the texture raster or the prelight colours carry alpha.

    In OmegaStudio

    Opens Packfile.dat on its own or in place inside a disc image. Entries whose payload starts with !CMP are zlib streams with a little-endian unpacked size. The archive is read-only. GameCube and PS2 .trm and PSP .psp models render with their textures and play every animation they carry; the animations export with the model to glTF. .tex texture dictionaries decode per platform. .fsb sound banks, the .thp movie, .col, .wcol, .path and .dat show their details only.
